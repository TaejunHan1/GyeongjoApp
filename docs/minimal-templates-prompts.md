## 미니멀 템플릿 A — Line Frame (Wedding edition)

```
A truly minimal modern Korean WEDDING invitation card, portrait 1024x1400 px. Warm and joyful, NOT solemn. NOT a funeral notice.

Background: warm cream / champagne white (#FBF7EC). Lots of whitespace.

ONLY four elements on the entire card. No other text, no other decoration:

1. A LARGE color photograph of a young Korean couple in wedding attire, about 65% of card width, positioned in the upper-center:
   - Bride in soft white wedding dress, gentle smile
   - Groom in modern slim cream-or-dove-gray suit, warm soft expression
   - Warm golden-hour natural light, soft pastel champagne tones (NOT black and white, NOT desaturated)
   - Romantic, sunlit, joyful mood
   - Photo framed by a clean rectangle with thin 1.5px warm-charcoal outline (#3A2E22), subtly rounded corners (8px)

2. Below the photo, generous whitespace, then names in elegant thin Korean serif (Nanum Myeongjo style) with wide letter-spacing, color warm charcoal #3A2E22:
   "한 준 서  ♡  김 은 재"
   (the ♡ in muted gold #C9A96E, smaller than the names)

3. Below the names, smaller Korean text in italic thin serif: "결혼합니다" (color warm gold #C9A96E)

4. Generous whitespace, then date in modern thin Korean type:
   "2026 . 06 . 14"
   (centered, color warm charcoal)

THAT'S IT. NO header text. NO venue. NO floor info. NO footer message. NO corner ornaments. NO border frame around the card. NO closing lines. NO additional decoration.

The entire card is just: warm cream background + one large warm color photo + names + "결혼합니다" + date. Maximum whitespace. Pure minimalism.

NO flowers, NO leaves, NO botanical illustration. NO geometric ornaments. NO frames or borders. Just clean typography + photo on warm cream background.

CRITICAL: this must clearly look like a WEDDING invitation (warm color photo, gold accents, joyful), NOT a funeral notice. Use warm cream and gold tones. AVOID cold gray, B&W photo, austere atmosphere.

Print-ready flat design.
```

## 변경 포인트 (요소 대폭 축소)

이전엔 요소가 너무 많았음 → 4가지로 축소:

**남기는 것**
1. ✅ 사진 (크게)
2. ✅ 이름 (한준서 ♡ 김은재)
3. ✅ "결혼합니다"
4. ✅ 날짜

**제거한 것**
- ❌ 상단 헤더 "우리 결혼합니다 · WE ARE GETTING MARRIED"
- ❌ 장소 (그랜드 하얏트 서울)
- ❌ 층/홀 (로즈홀 · 2층)
- ❌ 시간 (토요일 오전 11시 30분)
- ❌ 푸터 문구 (두 사람의 새 출발에…)
- ❌ 사각 외곽 프레임
- ❌ 모서리 장식
- ❌ 하단 마무리 라인 + 다이아몬드

**핵심 키워드**: `ONLY four elements`, `THAT'S IT`, `Maximum whitespace`, `NO border frame`, `NO corner ornaments` 강조해서 ChatGPT가 또 장식 못 끼워넣게 차단.
